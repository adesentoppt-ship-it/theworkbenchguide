TedsWoodworking Review (2026): Is 16,000 Plans Worth It for Beginners?

The Workbench Guide · Updated October 2026

Disclosure: This is an independent review, not the official TedsWoodworking website. If you buy through links on this page we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It does not change our assessment.

Short answer: TedsWoodworking is a large digital library of woodworking plans. It suits beginners and hobbyists who want lots of project ideas in one place for a one-time price. It is less useful if you only need one specific, professionally drafted plan.

What's inside

According to the official site, membership includes:

  • About 16,000 project plans with step-by-step instructions, schematics and cutting/material lists.
  • Projects of every size: large furniture, small crafts, outdoor projects (sheds, benches, planters) and workshop builds.
  • Video lessons on techniques and on building projects.
  • New plans each month added to the member area.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Huge variety for a one-time price
  • Good for ideas and simple beginner builds
  • Material lists help you plan your shopping
  • Instant digital access
Cons
  • Quality and drawing style vary between plans; some look like older scanned material
  • The sheer size can feel overwhelming at first
  • No substitute for hands-on tool-safety instruction
  • Optional upsells are offered after purchase

Who should buy it – and who shouldn't

Good fit: you're starting out, you like trying different projects, and you'd rather pay once than buy plans one by one.

Not a good fit: you need one specific, precise plan (for example a complex cabinet), or you prefer learning in person.

Alternatives

  • Free plans from tool brands and hardware stores – fine for a single simple project.
  • Individual paid plans from independent designers – better if you need one high-quality plan.
  • Local woodworking classes – best for learning technique and safety.

Verdict

If you want a big library of ideas and beginner-friendly plans for a single payment, TedsWoodworking is worth a look, especially with the refund window as a safety net. If you only need one precise plan, a single high-quality plan will serve you better.
